Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This policy explains what information Coral Shopping collects when you shop with us, how we use it, and how we protect it.

1. Information we collect

When you create an account or place an order, we collect:

  • Your name, phone number and delivery address
  • Your email address, used for account sign-in
  • Order details, such as items purchased, order value and order status
  • Custom shopping requests you submit, including your budget

We do not collect or store your bank card or account details. Payment is by direct bank transfer, made by you to our account.

2. How we use your information

We use your information to:

  • Process and deliver your orders
  • Verify payments and keep you updated on order status
  • Respond to custom shopping requests and support questions
  • Improve the products and service we offer

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

3. Who can see your information

Your order and account information is only accessible to you and to Coral Shopping's store administrators, who need it to process and deliver your orders.

4. Cookies and similar technology

This website uses only the technology needed to make it work, such as keeping you signed in on the ecommerce store. We do not currently use advertising or tracking cookies.

5. Data retention

We keep your order history so you can view past orders from your Dashboard. If you would like your account and associated data deleted, contact us and we will action this, subject to any records we are required to keep for legitimate business or legal reasons.

6. Your rights

You can ask us at any time to tell you what information we hold about you, correct anything that's inaccurate, or delete your account. Reach out using the contact details below.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the store grows. The version posted here is the one currently in effect.
